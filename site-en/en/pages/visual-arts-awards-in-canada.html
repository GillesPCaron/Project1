Aimia/AGO Photography Prize

Bourse Plein sud

Offert aux artistes comptabilisant 10 ans et moins de carrière professionnelle. Les trois membres qui composent le jury de la Bourse Plein sud proposent la candidature de trois artistes chacun pour un total de 9 candidatures mises en nomination. Un nouveau jury indépendant est nommé chaque année, composé d’intervenants du milieu de l’art et d’artistes reconnus pour leurs compétences en art actuel et contemporain. Les noms des membres du jury sont connus le jour du dévoilement du lauréat.

Les neuf artistes proposés par les membres du jury sont mis en nomination publiquement au printemps de chaque année. De ces neuf nominations, trois finalistes sont retenus par le jury sur la qualité de leur projet d’exposition proposé et de leur parcours artistique. Après délibérations, le lauréat final se mérite la Bourse Plein sud d’un montant de 5 000 $, alors qu’un montant de 500 $ est remis aux deux autres finalistes pour leur participation.

Molson Prizes

These prizes are for individuals. Candidates must be Canadian citizens or permanent residents of Canada, as defined by Citizenship and Immigration Canada. They do not need to be residing in Canada.

To be nominated, candidates must have made a substantial and distinguished contribution over a significant period. In the words of the deed of the gift, the prizes are intended “to encourage Canadians of outstanding achievement in the fields of the Arts, the Humanities or the Social Sciences to make further contribution to the cultural or intellectual heritage of Canada....” These terms of reference are interpreted as follows:

  • candidates must have clearly demonstrated “outstanding achievement” in order to be nominated
  • “further contribution” indicates that the candidates should still be active and productive.

With the support of the Canadian Art Foundation, is a unique initiative to help nurture and support promising new artists in the early stages of their careers; a time when they need both recognition and financial support.

The three jury panels then select one national winner and two honourable mentions from the 15 semi-finalists.

The Hnatyshyn Foundation

Its programs are funded by donations from government, foundations, corporations and individuals.

The Foundation began awarding grants to developing artists in the performing arts in 2005 and has since expanded its programming to include awards and residencies for emerging and mid-career artists and curators working in the visual arts.
