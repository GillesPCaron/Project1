Our specialized Google search consoles

 

These consoles allow you to search through the websites of art dealers (art galleries, virtual galleries and art brokers sites) of a given location (district, city, province/state, etc..).  Always remember the possibilities and limitations of these tools in order to maximize your results.

We currently cover, in whole or in part, the websites of commercial art dealers in several countries including Canada, the United States, France and Britain.
