How much will it cost to acquire a work at auction?

Instructions

  1. Enter the amount obtained or anticipated as auction price ('hammer price')
  2. Fill the amounts associated with the " Costs to consider "
    • In the case of Fixed costs , enter the amount in the currency of your choice (CAD, USD or Euro)
    • In the case of Variable costs , enter the percentage (%) applicable to your situation (information provided by the auction house)

Calculations are performed automatically.
