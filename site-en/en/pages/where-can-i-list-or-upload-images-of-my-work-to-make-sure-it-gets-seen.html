Where can I publish/upload photos of my work to be seen?

 

Starting a career in the visual arts is never easy.  If you are not yet represented by a gallery, here are some ways to exhibit and promote your work:

1. Social Media & Online Presence

  • Instagram – One of the best platforms for visual artists. Post regularly and use hashtags like #emergingartist, #contemporaryart, and #painting.
  • Facebook – Create an artist page and join local and international artist groups.
  • Pinterest – Great for attracting art lovers and driving traffic to your website.
  • TikTok & YouTube – If you're comfortable with video, show your painting process to engage viewers.

2. Art Platforms & Online Marketplaces

  • Saatchi Art – A well-known online gallery where emerging artists can sell their work.
  • Singulart – A curated platform that supports emerging artists.
  • Artmajeur – An easy-to-use platform for selling and displaying artwork.
  • Etsy – Ideal for selling small paintings or prints.
  • Redbubble / Society6 – Good for selling reproductions on various products.

3. Local Opportunities & Art Events

  • Art fairs & markets – Look for local art fairs where you can showcase and sell your work.
  • Call for artists – Check with community art centers, coffee shops, and libraries that feature local artists.
  • Art competitions – Winning or even being shortlisted in a contest can increase visibility.  Here are a listing of opportunities for Canadian.

4. Build Your Own Website or Portfolio

  • Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress – Create a professional portfolio to showcase your work.
  • Behance – Good for presenting a polished portfolio and connecting with the art community.
