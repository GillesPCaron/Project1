Biography of Gilles Caron

 

Gilles Caron was born on December 27, 1945, in St-Martin, Beauce, Quebec. A retired university librarian, administrator, author, musician, and collector of Canadian and international art, he pursued his career at Université Laval and the Université du Québec à Chicoutimi (UQAC). He is also known for his involvement in the library and information science community, his musical compositions, and his website, Les Collectionneurs Associés (Associated Art Collectors).

Education

Gilles Caron began elementary school in September 1952 and completed it in June 1959. He then began his classical college education in September 1959 at Séminaire St-Georges de Beauce, completing this course of study in June 1967 at Externat Classique St-Jean-Eudes in Quebec City.

Between January 1965 and August 1966, he was a member of the musical group Les Bacheliers.

He began a bachelor's degree in political science in September 1967 and completed it in June 1970. He subsequently pursued a Master of Library Science (MLS) degree at McGill University, beginning in September 1973 and graduating in June 1975.

Professional Career

In July 1970, Gilles Caron began working at the Université Laval Library (BUL) as an advisor for the development of documentary resources in the social sciences. He remained in this position until February 7, 1977, when he left Laval to join UQAC.

At UQAC, he held several positions:

  • 1977–1984: Assistant to the Director of Library Services, responsible for collection selection and management.
  • 1984–1989: Assistant to the Vice-Rector for Teaching and Research, with responsibilities in strategic planning, international cooperation, and communications.
  • 1989–1990: Assistant to the Rector, responsible for strategic planning and international cooperation.
  • 1990–2008: Director of the Paul-Émile-Boulet Library at UQAC.

He retired on January 1, 2008.

Awards and Achievements

Gilles Caron received several distinctions, including:

  • The University of Quebec's 2001 Career Award in Management.
  • Membership in the University of Quebec's Circle of Excellence, 2001 edition.
  • The University of Quebec's 2007 Award of Excellence in Management (Achievement category).

He contributed to several major projects at the Paul-Émile-Boulet Library at UQAC. These included the design of a model focused on optimizing customer service, the development of an information literacy training program, and the implementation of an integrated management platform for the Virtual Library.

He was also responsible for initially hosting the collection Les Classiques des Sciences Sociales (The Classics of the Social Sciences) on the website of the Université du Québec à Chicoutimi Library. This collection was created and developed by Jean-Marie Tremblay.

Teaching and Professional Involvement

Between 1970 and 1983, he taught courses at Université Laval and UQAC, particularly in research methodology, social survey techniques, and documentary research in political and social sciences.

He was actively involved in numerous professional associations and committees, including ASTED, the Corporation des bibliothécaires professionnels du Québec (Corporation of Professional Librarians of Quebec), the Centre québécois des relations internationales (CQRI), the Association des employés cadre de l'UQAC (UQAC Management Employees Association), and the Association du personnel non-syndiqué de l'UQAC (UQAC Non-Unionized Staff Association), of which he was the founding president.

Passionate about music, he composes his own pieces and shares his creations on SoundCloud. A collector of visual art for more than forty years, he launched the website Les Collectionneurs Associés (Associated Art Collectors) in 2005, a platform designed to facilitate the exchange of information and artworks among collectors. More recently, he has developed an interest in pochoir color reproduction, a technique he is seeking to promote and bring to wider public attention.
