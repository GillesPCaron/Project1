Remember:

  • Possibilities in framing are limitless. A physical presence with your work remains the best means to ensure a product that meets your expectations.
  • Are your needs cosmetic and/or long-term protection? The answer to this question will help your framer to better focus on what he can propose.
  • Museum quality? The  Fine Art Trade Guild (FATG)  defines museum quality as a "process intend to be fully reversible up to 35 years, which means that the framed work can be returned to its former state, i.e. prior to framing, at any time, assuming that the artwork is not inherently unstable."  All your work will probably not justify such a level of framing quality and the associated costs.
  • Available resources?   Canadian Conservation Institute , the standards and terminology proposed by FATG.
  • You wish to see framed any photo (work of art, character, etc.) already available in digital format?  More and more companies are offering this service over the Internet. ​
  • See our "Experts advices" on framing.
