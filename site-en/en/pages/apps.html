Best mobile apps for the art collector

 

Preliminary remarks:

  • Only mobile apps accessible on smartphones (iPhone, Galaxy, etc.) and tablets with iOS (Apple) or Android (Google) operating systems are listed here.
  • We have not retained here any app constituting the mobile replica on smartphone or tablet of the traditional web page of cultural institutions such as museums or art galleries. The apps developed by the latter are easily located on the website of these establishments or following a simple search on Google.
  • We have not selected any mobile application whose only interest is to offer works for sale online.   There are hundreds of them on the Internet.
  • Finally, the movement (additions/deletions) in the "apps" sector makes it extremely difficult to keep our list up to date.  We apologize in advance.
