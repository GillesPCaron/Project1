Individuals and companies offering to produce an estimate of the market value of your works of art after the submission of an online summary dossier

 

Individuals and companies identified here offer you, for pricing, the ability to obtain a basic evaluation following the filing of a description file online.

ATTENTION: This type of evaluation is of interest only to get an overview of the market value of your works. For a complete list of individuals or companies able to produce a certified professional assessment of your work as required by insurance companies or government agencies, see our page "Individuals and firms providing assessment services of your works of art".

Remember:

  • Prior to selling a work of art, assume that you are usually able by yourself to estimate the approximate value of your artwork. See our section on "Market Value Range" (MVR) in our FAQ.
  • Recognized auction house, as well as good gallery owners, are usually able to produce valid estimates of the market value of your works.   Remember that you can, by using our site, join by email several, if not all, companies (galleries or auction houses) mentioned on a given page. For example, you want to get an evaluation of a work you want to sell by auction? Here's how to  proceed:
    • go to our page "Auction houses";
    • Select the companies of your choice by checking the box preceding the name;
    • "Click" to "Contact";
    • Complete the form by submitting as much information as possible about your work (artist, size, medium, etc);
    • "Click" to "Submit". 
    Your email will be automatically forwarded to all companies you have selected. Interested companies will be able to contact you afterward to answer your request or to obtain additional information. Simple and efficient.
  • Mearto, a European evaluation firm, maintains an up-to-date directory of individuals and companies active in evaluation.
