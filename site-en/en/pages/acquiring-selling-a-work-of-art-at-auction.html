Simulators of the costs associated with a buy/sell transaction at auction

 

This tool will help you, through informations available to you, to simulate the breakdown of the diverse costs associated with a buy/sell transaction at auction.

How to proceed?

  1. Fill the form with all the parameters you have to consider.   This information, a percentage (%) or a number (monetary value), will come from three (3) different sources:
    • Yourself (re: transportation costs);
    • Your auction house (re: seller's premium, buyer's premium, insurance, etc.);
    • Your government for taxes.
  2. Write the anticipated or obtained adjudication price ("Hammer Price").
  3. "Click" on "Actualiser"

The tool will produce a monetary breakdown of the resulting amounts between the different partners (buyer, seller, auction house and government).

Our simulators are designed to address the four (4) following questions:

  • How much can I expect from the sale of my artwork at auction?

  • How much will it cost to acquire a work at auction?

  • How much should I sell a work at auction if I expect to recoup the equivalent of my total cost of acquisition?

  • How to return to the "hammer price" from the displayed sale price?
